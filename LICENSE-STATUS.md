# License status

No external distribution license has been selected for this repository. The repository provides no general permission to copy, redistribute, sublicense, or publish its contents.

[ADR-0004](docs/adr/0004-internal-license-and-provenance-policy.md) is Accepted and classifies the repository as internal research material. Authorized internal build, test, review, evidence extraction, and research use must follow the source-specific restrictions and provenance register.

External publication or transfer remains blocked until a later accepted license-selection ADR confirms rights holders, closes dependency/data/archive/generated-artifact provenance, packages obligations and notices, and records the required approvals.

The task decision is `R0-GOV-002` in `docs/tasks/backlog.json`; the operational policy is [license-and-provenance-policy.md](docs/handoff/license-and-provenance-policy.md).
